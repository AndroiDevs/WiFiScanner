Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

At WiFi Scanner, we take your privacy extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and process your data when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Access and Process

Camera & Live Scanning: We request camera permission exclusively to scan Wi-Fi QR codes in real time on your device. Video frames are analyzed in temporary memory by the on-device barcode reader and are immediately discarded. We never record, save, or transmit camera images or video to external servers.

Gallery Photos: If you choose to scan a Wi-Fi QR code from an image stored on your device, the photo is accessed strictly on-demand using Android's system photo picker. The image is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded or shared.

Local Wi-Fi Network Storage: The app saves your scanned Wi-Fi network names (SSID), security types, and passwords locally in a secure, on-device SQLite database (Room). This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or cloud account.

In-App Purchases: All coin purchases and transactions are processed securely and directly by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, view, or store your credit card or financial information.

2. How We Use Information

All accessed permissions and data are used solely to facilitate instant Wi-Fi connections, maintain your saved network history, and enable optional password unlocks via Google Play purchases.

3. Third-Party Services

The app interacts with official Google Play services:

  • Google Play In-App Billing: Used to process purchases and manage virtual coin transactions securely under Google's standard privacy policies.

4. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because all Wi-Fi network records and scan histories are stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times. You can delete individual network entries directly within the Saved Wi-Fi history screen, or erase all app data at any time through your Android device's Settings > Apps > WiFi Scanner > Clear Storage.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is a general-purpose utility tool and is not intended for or directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

All network credentials and app settings reside within the secure private application sandbox provided by the Android operating system. No external network communication occurs other than standard Google Play purchase flows and public store package catalog queries.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
