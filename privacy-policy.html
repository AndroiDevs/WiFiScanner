Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

At WiFi Scanner, we take your privacy extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and process your data when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

Device Sensors & Camera: We request camera permissions strictly to scan WiFi QR codes dynamically and locally on your device. We never transmit camera footage or imagery to remote servers.

WiFi Connection Information: Our app stores your connected networks and scanned configurations locally in an offline Room SQLite database. This data never leaves your device.

In-App Purchases: All payments, transactions, and purchases of coins are securely processed entirely through Google Play Services Billing API. We do not store or process payment card details.

2. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used solely to facilitate WiFi connections, store scanning history, and verify active premium coin purchases via the official Google Play infrastructure.

3. Third-Party Services

We integrate official Google APIs to verify purchases:

  • Google Play Billing Services (to handle orders safely and securely).

4. Security

Your local network passwords and details are encrypted or securely compiled locally in your sandbox databases. No external network transmission is attempted except during essential Billing operations initiated by Google Play Services.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage users to check this page periodically for updates.
